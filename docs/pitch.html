The problem

A CCTV-and-server install costs about ₹2 lakh.

Small construction sites and factories don't have that. So nobody is watching the press, the pit or the fire exit — and the first anyone hears about a breach is after someone is hurt.

Why this is different

Every camera safety system on the market is a witness.
Ours is a guard.

Forensic

Documents the breach

Records what happened so you can review it later. The accident still happened.

Preventive

Stops the breach

Tracks where a person is heading, predicts the crossing 0.75 seconds early, and speaks a warning out loud. It logs a breach only when the warning failed.

Live on this phone — aeroplane mode on

Watch it happen.

  1. Phone propped at the hazard. Aeroplane mode, visibly on.
  2. Draw the danger zone. One drag on the screen.
  3. Walk toward it. It warns before the line is crossed.
  4. Cross anyway. Breach logged, with a photo.
  5. Open Alerts. Every warning and breach, with its photo.
  6. One tap — shift report, faces blurred, written on the phone.

The model ships inside the APK — 20 MB of weights, loaded from the phone's own storage. Switch the network off and nothing changes, because nothing was using it.

Using the phone as a phone

Speaker, camera, storage — the whole system is one handset.

  • The speaker is the safety device. A warning nobody hears is just a log entry. This is why the phone has to be in the room rather than in a server cupboard.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Only a photo of the moment a rule broke, kept here.
  • The log survives a lock, a reload, the app being swiped away. It is the site's log book, and it is on the site.

What it does not do yet

Said plainly.

  • No hard-hat or vest detection. The detector knows people and objects, not PPE. Same pipeline, fine-tuned model — but it isn't there today.
  • The report blurs the whole frame, not detected faces. Masking only detections makes anonymity depend on the detector succeeding, and it misses. This fails closed.
  • One camera, one angle. It watches what you point it at.
